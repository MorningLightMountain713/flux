How to read it. Each row is a capability. A light bar is the time it spent coming up, held below a prerequisite; the dark edge is the moment it came up; a faint bar after that is the state it is still in. A connector joins a capability to the prerequisite it was waiting on, drawn in the accent when both are on the critical chain. Blame lists capabilities by when they came up; own wait is the time between the capability first being asked for and its coming up.
